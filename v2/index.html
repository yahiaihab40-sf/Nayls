Put the nails on in 10 minutes

  1. Prepare your nails

    Push back the cuticles, buff the shine off each nail, then wipe it with alcohol so nothing sits under the glue.

  2. Match a nail to each finger

    Lay out the 24 nails and match one to each finger, starting with the thumbs, which take the widest sizes.

  3. Press and hold for 30 seconds

    Add a drop of glue or a tab, press the nail on from the cuticle down at an angle, and hold it firmly.

What if a nail is between two sizes?

Take the larger size and file its sides until it sits inside your nail’s edges.

Is one box of nails enough for both hands?

Yes. Ten fingers take ten nails, so the 24 in a box leave spares in every size.

Does every nail design come in every shape?

Yes. All 16 designs come in coffin, almond, stiletto, square and oval.

Are the nails vegan?

Yes. The nails, glue and tabs are vegan and cruelty-free.
