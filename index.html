The ritual

Salon nails in three steps

Applied with glue, a set lasts up to two weeks. Use the adhesive tabs for a few days of wear, then pop them off and wear them again.

Prepare

Push back cuticles, buff the shine off your natural nail, then wipe with alcohol so nothing sits between nail and glue.

Size

Lay out the 24 nails and match one to each finger. If you are between sizes, pick the larger one and file the sides.

Press

Add a drop of glue or a tab, press at an angle from the cuticle down, and hold firmly for 30 seconds. Done.

Size guide

Measure each nail across its widest point in millimetres and match it to the closest size below. Thumbs are usually 0–2, pinkies 8–9.
